TABULA III · NOTES

The rules, printed on the drawing.

  1. ×3.02 is measured, not drawn. Every number on a plate carries its source.
  2. A ceiling is never guessed: it is derived downward from the information limit, the mathematical limit, and the physical limit.
  3. What cannot be measured stays blank. A blank is never filled in — the filled-in stroke is the invented one.
  4. Every reading carries a timestamp. An expired reading is not a reading; it is a memory.
  5. When the channels disagree, the most careful answer wins. Stay in the game first.
  6. Failures enter the library with a date and the reason it was wrong. Dates are never edited; pages are never torn out.
  7. Limits are computed per person, not per account. The money is a person’s — tuition, rent, and the way out are inside it.
  8. When the state changes, last second’s judgement expires and is recomputed. Morning weather is not used for a night road.
  9. A strategy enters the arsenal signed with source and year. Being fielded is a separate decision — first, know in what weather it lives and in what weather it dies.
TABULA IV

Three things get measured.

Measuring the ceiling means measuring how much money is on the table today, and how much of it could be taken. Measuring yourself means measuring how much of it you have reached — the part still missing is money not yet taken. Boundaries are measured first so the hand knows how far it may open. A boundary exists to free the hand, not to shrink it.

Measure you — position and limit

First, your own book: where you stand, and how large a seat at this table is yours.

TERM — · ·

DYNAMIC — the limit is recomputed as your position changes; when the money’s job changes, the limit changes on the spot. LIMIT — the ceiling of the limit is the ceiling of a real life, not of an account number.

Example. ¥60,000 is next March’s tuition. It does not even qualify to enter — it is set aside first. Only what remains may talk about position size. Note: it is set aside not out of fear; that money already has an owner.

Measure the market — four channels, cross-checked

What others can do, we can do. The difference is information and judgement: state, order flow, volatility, and news causality arrive together and check each other before any judgement is made — that is why it is more accurate. And the judgement stays alive: when the market moves, it is recomputed on the spot.

TERM — · · · · ·

DYNAMIC — when the state changes, last second’s readings expire; all four channels re-read and re-check. LIMIT — judgement is capped by how fast the four channels can physically arrive together; if they cannot, no pretending to have seen clearly.

Example. At 23:40 an unverified rumour arrives. It is stamped unverified and enters the fast, unverified lane. Until the other three channels catch up, judgement walks around it — not disbelief; verification is simply not finished.

Measure itself — ceiling and error

The machine is measured too: how well it can possibly do, and — the weakest cell speaks for the whole.

TERM — · · · · ·

DYNAMIC — the error budget is recomputed with the state; worse weather, wider error, automatically more careful conclusions. LIMIT — the ceiling is derived downward from information, mathematics, and physics; the claim stops where the derivation stops.

Example. A reading whose error is so wide it cannot tell cold from cool may not be written down as a conclusion. The only conclusion available this time is: no move this time — and when the next reading sharpens, the conclusion changes with it, on the spot.

TABULA VIII

FOR THE CURIOUS · 普通人

What does it actually do for me?

It measures five things before anything moves: where you stand, your limit, this second’s weather, which strategy fits, and where the red line runs.

Take the ¥60,000 that is next March’s tuition: it never qualifies to enter, and it is set aside first. Only the rest is ever discussed.

It does not dream for you. It measures for you.

Can it lose my money?

No machine in a market is loss-proof, and this one is no exception. It does not promise returns.

What it does instead: before any move, the limit is computed from your real life — tuition, rent, and the way out are inside it. Once the boundary is measured, the hand knows how far it may open.

A boundary exists to free the hand, not to shrink it.

What happens after I leave my email?

The builder writes to you personally, about once a month, and you can unsubscribe in one click, any time.

No list-selling, no ad blasts, no pressure to buy anything. This is still the intent stage — leaving an email only says: when this exists, call me.

FOR RESEARCHERS · 研究者

How is the ceiling derived?

Downward from three places — the information limit, the mathematical limit, and the physical limit — and the tightest of the three binds.

Every conclusion is reported together with its error budget: the wider the error, the less the claim may say. The weakest cell gets its own line and is never hidden inside an average.

Can I see the failure library?

Yes. The failure library is among the first parts of this house to open — the three pages on Plate VII are specimens.

Each page carries a date, the cause, and the organ at fault. A verdict can be overturned, but only with new evidence and a non-author review — and the original page stays.

How is it different from other systems?

What others can do, we can do. The difference is the number of information channels, and how judgement is made.

State, order flow, volatility, and news causality arrive together and check each other before any judgement. If they disagree, the most careful answer wins. And the judgement stays alive: when the state changes, last second’s readings expire and everything is recomputed on the spot.

FOR INVESTORS · 投资人

Why personal-scale, not institutional?

Because a limit computed per person is something an institution cannot do — institutional limits are computed per account and per compliance rule.

One person’s tuition, rent, and way out differ from the next person’s. Computing those into the limit is why this machine was built for a person from day one.

What comes after the intent list?

Replies, one by one. First, a conversation about the one money question you most want measured — no blasts, no roadshow.

In registration order. If it is not a fit, it stops there, with no follow-up chasing.
